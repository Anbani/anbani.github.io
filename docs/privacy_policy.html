Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

At Anbani.ge, accessible from https://anbani.ge, the privacy of our visitors is a priority. This policy explains what information is collected, the third-party services we use, and how you control your consent. If you have questions, please contact us.

No accounts, local-first

Anbani.ge has no user accounts and no login. Preferences and data you create on the site — such as saved conversions and your dark-mode choice — are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). They are not sent to or stored on our servers.

Services and trackers we use

We use the following third-party services. Each may set cookies or similar identifiers and processes data under its own privacy policy. We have grouped them by the consent category they fall under (see Consent below):

  • Google AdSense (advertising) — ad selection, delivery and measurement, including the Google DART cookie. Privacy & ad settings
  • Google Analytics & Google Tag Manager (analytics) — measuring how the site is used. Policy
  • PostHog (analytics) — self-hosted product analytics at posthog.anbani.ge. Policy
  • RudderStack (analytics) — analytics event delivery. Policy
  • Meta / Facebook SDK (social) — social plugins such as the Facebook Page and Share buttons. Policy
  • TOP.GE (analytics) — Georgian website traffic counter. top.ge

Consent (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) that follows the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF v2.2). When you first visit, a banner lets you Consent, Do not consent, or Manage options.

The advertising, analytics and social services listed above load only after you grant the relevant consent. Until you choose, they stay off, and the core site (the converter, learning pages, keyboard, calendar and so on) works without them. Visitors outside these regions are not shown the banner and these services load by default; you can still control cookies through your browser settings.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by reopening the consent options (the privacy/consent link provided by the banner) or by clearing this site's cookies. You can also opt out of personalized Google advertising at Google Ad Settings.

Log files

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard log information when you visit, which may include your IP address, browser type, Internet Service Provider, date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. This is used to analyze trends and administer the site, and is not linked to personally identifiable information.

GDPR data protection rights

If you are in the EEA/UK, you are entitled to the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request copies of your personal data.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Restrict processing — request that we restrict processing, under certain conditions.
  • Object to processing — object to our processing, under certain conditions.
  • Data portability — request transfer of your data, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

CCPA privacy rights

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, to request deletion of that data, and to opt out of its sale. We do not sell your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

Children's information

Protecting children online is a priority. Anbani.ge does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child provided such information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
